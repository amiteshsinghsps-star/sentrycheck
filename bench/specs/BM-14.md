# BM-14: Replicated Scans

## Objective
Measure verdict stability and detect execution flakiness or non-determinism across duplicate live runs on the pilot corpus.

## Protocol
- Live evaluation arm repeated per case (`[live, live]`, 14 runs across 7 cases).
- Any verdict flip between duplicate runs flags non-determinism.

## Findings
- **7/7 cases stable** (14 runs, single host, Wilson 95% CI approximately 0.65 to 1.00).
- Zero verdict flips observed across repeated executions.
- Two runs per case provides preliminary pilot evidence; broader multi-host replication is documented in the claims ladder (L4).
